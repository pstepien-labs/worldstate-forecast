**Correction to edition 01 (ed.01 G3-003, block L):** edition 01 described the tariffs as an authorisation that the president "may" use. The enrolled text (G3-001) says the President "shall" raise duties within 30 days, to a rate "up to" 500% / 100% (no minimum), with a general waiver (Sec. 115). ASSESSMENT: legally the act is a mandate with a ceiling and a waiver path; in practice the administration can comply with a low rate, a waiver, or not at all — Greer and Wright (G3-002, G3-003) leave the choice to the President.

**Calendar consequence (calculation):** enactment 18.09 → 30-day deadline 18.10.2026; the 10-day prior notice to Congress (Sec. 113(g)(1)) would have to be submitted by about 08.10 for duties effective on 18.10. No such notice and no waiver certification found by 07.10.
