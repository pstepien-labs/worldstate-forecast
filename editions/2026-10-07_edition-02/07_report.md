**Notation conventions**

- **FACT** — an event with a date (dd.mm.yyyy), a publisher and a record identifier in `02_facts/` (e.g. G1-001). The full record holds the URL, the source rating in the Admiralty code (A–F / 1–6), the perspective (W Western · A actor's side · T third party) and the PIR link. For key facts the source rating is given in brackets, e.g. [B/2]. Records A-01…A-14, B-01…B-14 and C-01…C-15 were fetched by the forecasting lenses in stage 04 (tables "Additional facts" in `04_forecasts_*.md`), R-01…R-03 by the red team (`05_red_team.md` §7); they have the full record format. Records dated after 07.10.2026 are marked **(after the state date)**; they are facts, but not state values.
- Event status: **DONE** carried out and confirmed · **DECL** declaration or announcement · **DISP** disputed. A government statement (Washington, Moscow, Beijing, Tehran, Brussels, Warsaw) is a fact about a statement, not about an event.
- **ASSESSMENT** — inference by the author of the report, always with analytic confidence (low / medium / high). Confidence is the quality of the evidence, not a probability.
- **FORECAST** — numerical probabilities appear only in section H and in the annex. The official forecast is **AGG_RT** (the mean of lenses A, B, C after accepted red-team adjustments). Verbal text follows methodology §10: almost ruled out 1–5% · very unlikely 5–20% · unlikely 20–45% · roughly even chances 45–55% · likely 55–80% · very likely 80–95% · almost certain 95–99%.
- Disputed events: two interpretations, marked (1) and (2), not decided without evidence.
- Facts carried over from edition 01 after confirmation in stage 02 carry the note "confirmed unchanged, dd.mm.yyyy".
- Outlets on the no-republish list (Russian state media, sanctioned outlets) are paraphrased as "according to state agency X" and never quoted.
- Comparisons with the crowd and prediction markets are not part of this report (methodology §6); they are in the separate file `07_annex_benchmarks.md`.

## Executive summary

**Iran: from a suspension of strikes with direct contact to a war of attrition at sea.** FACT: no US strike on Iranian land territory has been found since 01.09.2026 (stage 01, Q-0041; G1-010). The US blockade of Iranian ports continues: the 130th vessel was redirected on 05.10, and over 12 weeks 3 vessels were disabled and 13 destroyed (CENTCOM via gCaptain, 05.10.2026; G1-007) [B/2]. Iran answered with a formal rule: on 05.10 the IRGC Navy said ships may use only the "northern route" with IRGC clearance, and ships on the southern route "will be targeted or hit a mine" (Fars via Shahrara News; G1-004) [C/3]. Between 01.10 and 06.10 at least 10 tankers were struck in and near Hormuz; UKMTO and JMIC name no attacker (gCaptain, Ship & Bunker; G1-002) [B/2]; on 06.10 12 crew were injured on the MT On Peace (Anadolu citing India's MEA; G1-003). Diplomacy has fallen back to indirect messages through Qatar (28.09, 30.09, 06.10; G4-001, G4-006) [B/2], and in Tehran the mandate itself is disputed: an impeachment motion against FM Araghchi was registered on 06.10 (Euronews Persian, The National; G4-009). Washington ordered a third carrier group and 9–10k personnel to the region, with three carriers and two amphibious groups planned by the end of November (Al Jazeera citing a US official, WSJ, AP; G1-009) [B/2]. After the state date Trump said on 08.10 that the US would not strike Iran before the 03.11 midterms (Gulf News; A-03) [B/3]. ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): the land ceasefire holds, but the sea is not quiet; the risk of escalation has been explicitly moved by Washington to the weeks after 03.11.

**Oil near 100 USD in futures, much tighter in physical barrels; diesel is the binding shortage.** FACT: December Brent settled at 100.20 USD/bbl on 07.10 (Bloomberg via Rigzone; G2-002) [B/2], while Dated Brent spot stood at 113.96 on 29.09 (EIA via FRED; G2-003) [A/1]. The IMF PortWatch AIS series recorded 0–5 Hormuz transits a day between 23.09 and 04.10, 7-day mean 2.7 (G1-001) [A/2], while banks estimate Middle East crude exports at 75–98% of pre-war levels, largely AIS-dark (Bloomberg via Rigzone; G2-005; G1-006) [B/3]. Iran loaded no crude in September (Bessent; Kpler; Vortexa; G2-012) [B/2]. A record European diesel price of 2.24 EUR/l (G2-011) set off a chain: a US threat of a diesel export ban unless France and Germany released stocks (Reuters sources, 01.10; G2-008) [B/3], a G7 release of up to 100 mn bbl (02.10; G2-009) and an IEA decision to accelerate releases (07.10; G2-010). After the state date OFAC issued General License 135 authorising imports of Russian-origin diesel, including into the US, until 07.04.2027, after a Putin–Trump call (OFAC; NBC; R-02) [A/2].

**The Saudi–Houthi war has become its own front.** FACT: Houthi strikes killed 3 people and injured 36 at Riyadh and Abha airports on 06–07.10 (GACA via Reuters and Al Jazeera; G1-018) [B/2] — the first Saudi fatalities on Saudi soil in this escalation recorded in this project. After the state date, a missile on King Khalid airport on 08.10 killed a Saudia pilot and two other Saudi citizens (GACA, 09.10, via Al Jazeera; R-01) [B/2]. The Saudi–Pakistani–Turkish "Mecca Alliance" was activated on 05.10 with a rapid deployment of Pakistani and Turkish forces (Al Jazeera, Euronews; G1-020) [B/2]; Yemeni government forces opened an offensive at Dhubab and Mocha on 04.10, with disputed results (Reuters; AP; G1-015, G1-016) [B/3]. The US stays out "for now" (Axios 04.10; Rubio 05.10; G1-021). Traffic through Bab el-Mandeb did not fall: 22–37 transits a day (PortWatch; G1-012) [A/2].

**USA–China: time bought, legal texts unchanged; H.R. 5334 forces a visible choice by 18.10.** FACT: Bessent (23.09) and MOFCOM (28.09) announced the extension of the Kuala Lumpur arrangement to 10.01.2027 (NBC; Xinhua; G3-007, G3-010) [A/1], but the MOFCOM text does not name rare earths, the legal end date of the rare-earth suspension is still 10.11.2026 (G2-041) [B/2], and no US Federal Register notice formalising the extension was found by 07.10 (G3-011). The enrolled text of H.R. 5334 says the President "shall" raise duties within 30 days of 18.09 to a rate "up to" 500% on Russia and 100% on qualifying buyers, with a waiver (congress.gov; G3-001) [A/1]. USTR Greer will "name names" but leaves the action "for the president to decide" (Semafor; G3-002) [B/2]. Beijing has said nothing on the law since 19.09 (G3-059). ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): both capitals keep their levers cocked; GL 135 (R-02) now points the administration toward easing energy trade with Moscow nine days before its own statutory deadline.

**Russia prices the war upward.** FACT: Putin at Valdai (01.10) repeated his terms — Ukraine leaves the rest of Donbas and renounces alliances (kremlin.ru; G4-019) [A/1]; on 07.10 Deputy FM Ryabkov said there are "no current plans" for the US-proposed trilateral format (Interfax; G4-022) [B/1]. The 2027 draft budget raises defence to 17.15 trn RUB from 13.57 trn in the old plan and puts the 2026 deficit at 3.2% of GDP against 1.6% in the law (Fontanka, Kommersant; G3-016) [B/2]. High oil prices (Urals 92.08 USD/bbl in IX) quintupled the budget-rule FX purchases to 12.7 bn RUB a day from 07.10 (MinFin via Vedomosti; G3-019) [B/1]. On 07.10 one of the largest combined strikes on Ukraine killed at least 24 people (Kyiv Post, NBC, Anadolu; G1-047) [B/2]. The front balance is disputed: DeepState shows a Russian net gain of 154 km² in IX, ISW a Ukrainian net gain (G1-043, G1-044) — (1) and (2) in section B. After the state date a Putin–Trump call took place on 09.10 (UNN relaying the Kremlin; C-01) [B/3].

**NATO's flank moved south-east and toward nuclear rhetoric; the US posture decision moved to December.** FACT: a Russian Mi-8 was in Polish airspace for 42 s on 23.09 (DO RSZ via Defence24; G1-024) [A/2]; no further violation from Russia or Belarus was found to 07.10 (G1-025). On 05–06.10 three merchant ships were sunk or set on fire in the Romanian and Bulgarian EEZs, two crew died; no government attributed the attacks (Sofia Globe, Al Jazeera, Anadolu; G1-031, G1-032) [B/2]. Russia sent NATO a note on Kaliningrad and Putin warned of nuclear use if the exclave were cut off (The Moscow Times citing Reuters, 01.10; G1-029) [B/2]. Poland's deputy defence minister said the US base would be in western Poland with about 5,000 soldiers and no decision before December (TVN24, Defence24, 05.10; G1-037) [B/2]; Hegseth hinted at shifting forces from Europe to the Western Hemisphere and the posture review is now expected to end in December (Washington Times, 04.10; G1-038) [B/3]. No Article 4 request in 2026 (G1-036).

**Energy and money in Poland.** FACT: the CPN III package has been in force since 03.10 — VAT on fuel 8%, excise at the EU minimum, daily maximum prices (Forsal, Bankier; G2-026) [B/2]; the e-petrol national diesel average fell from a record 9.14 PLN/l (30.09) to 7.78 (07.10) (e-petrol via TVN24; G2-027) [B/2]. The Commission says the VAT cut is incompatible with the VAT Directive (TVN24 citing RMF FM; G2-028). Polish gas storage is 99.2% full, EU storage 72.83% — the lowest for the date since 2011 (PAP/GIE; Global Energy Flow; G2-016, G2-017) [C/2]. CPI in September 4.0% y/y (GUS; G3-039) [A/1]; the MPC kept 3.75% but hardened its wording (Bankier; G3-040). EUR/PLN 4.3797 on 07.10 (NBP; G3-045) [A/1], USD/PLN +2.5% in the window while the US 10-year yield reached 5.356% intraday, the highest since IV 2002 (CNBC; FRED; G3-035) [B/2].

**For Poland.** ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): in two weeks no factual state that decides between the scenarios has changed — Hormuz is closed in AIS data, there is no agreement on Iran or Ukraine, and the US–China truce was extended without settling rare earths. What changed is the weight of the crisis signals: Saudi fatalities, the tanker-strike wave, ships sunk in NATO members' EEZs and a US build-up timed for after 03.11; the favourable signals weakened (no second direct US–Iran round; Moscow declines the trilateral). For Warsaw the decisive dates have moved: the US base and force posture to December, the energy and inflation test to the winter, and the nearest hinge — US policy toward Iran — to the days after 03.11. The base scenario "Protracted crisis without resolution" remains the most likely; the distribution and the 88 forecasts are in section H and the annex.

## A. Strategic goals of the great powers

### USA

FACT: Washington runs three instruments of pressure on Iran at once and has used none to a decision. Military: the port blockade (130 vessels redirected by 05.10; G1-007) [B/2] and a build-up — the Theodore Roosevelt carrier group and the Makin Island amphibious group left on 27–28.09, three carriers and two amphibious groups around Iran planned by the end of November (G1-009) [B/2]; deliberations at Camp David on Iran and Yemen options, with strike options reported for "after the elections" (Iran International 03.10, Al Jazeera Arabic 06.10, CBS/Fox via 00_plan; G1-010) [C/3]. Diplomatic: only indirect exchanges through Qatar (G4-001, G4-006); Vance (06–07.10) demands a "meaningful" reduction of enrichment capacity, which experts read as softer than zero enrichment (Al Jazeera, Al-Monitor; G4-007) [B/2]; Trump (07.10): the war ends "the nice way or the not-so-nice way" (G4-008). Economic: Iran loaded no crude in IX (G2-012). Toward Russia and its buyers: H.R. 5334 obliges the President to act by 18.10, with a waiver (G3-001) [A/1]; no OFAC Russia designation 24.09–07.10 (G3-026) [A/1]. Toward China: the truce moved to 10.01.2027 (G3-007, G3-010); the "30-for-30" tariff lists are recommendations without a start date (Greer, 01.10; G3-063) [B/2]. Toward Europe: a threat of a US diesel export ban (01.10), dropped on 02.10 after the G7 release (G2-008, G2-009) [B/2]; Hegseth (04.10) hints at shifting forces to the Western Hemisphere (G1-038) [B/3]; requests that allies finance US presence — Lithuania offers to pay for a base (LRT, Bloomberg, 05–06.10; G1-040) [B/2]. Withdrawal from Iraq: the Operation Inherent Resolve mission ended on 30.09 (Atlantic Council, The National; G1-011) [B/2]. Domestic constraint: Trump's approval at 32%, equal to the record low (Reuters/Ipsos via The Hill, 30.09–05.10; G4-045) [B/2]; the US 10-year yield at a 24-year high (G3-035).

ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): the edition 01 pattern holds — leverage accumulated, use deferred to dates after 03.11 (Iran strikes, posture review). The one deadline Washington cannot defer without a formal act is H.R. 5334 (18.10). What is new is that the administration asks allies to carry the cost of its constraints (European diesel stocks, Polish and Lithuanian offers to fund bases) and, after the state date, opened an economic channel with Moscow on diesel (R-02).

### China

FACT: Beijing secured the extension of the Kuala Lumpur arrangement to 10.01.2027 — suspension of the US 24% reciprocal tariff, the US 50% affiliates rule and "related Chinese export controls", and the s.301 maritime measures (MOFCOM, 28.09; G3-010) [A/1] — without naming rare earths and without amending the legal end date of the rare-earth suspension (10.11.2026; G2-041) [B/2]. It agreed to coal purchases for 2027–28 and to tariff-cut recommendations on about 30 bn USD of goods each way (G3-008, G3-010). On Taiwan, Xi asked the US to keep its "correct position of opposing Taiwan independence" (PRC MFA, 25.09; G4-076) [A/1]. In parallel Beijing hardened toward the EU — an anti-dumping probe on EU p-nitrotoluene (03.10; G3-051) [B/2] and a refusal of voluntary curbs on hybrid-car exports (FT via CnEVPost; G3-052) [B/3] — and pressed the Philippines in the air at Scarborough (03.10; G1-055) [B/2]. The PRC MFA welcomed Putin's attendance at APEC Shenzhen (CGTN, 03.10; G4-027). Chinese independent refiners lost Iranian crude and pay up to +18 USD/bbl over Brent for Iraqi grades (The Insider citing Bloomberg; G2-035) [B/3]. The latest PRC statement on H.R. 5334 dates from 19.09: China "reserves the right to take all necessary measures" (MOFCOM via Xinhua; G3-059) [A/1].

ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): China turned the summit into a time extension that keeps its raw-materials lever legally intact. It separates fronts: stability with Washington, friction with Brussels and Manila. The Iran blockade now costs Chinese refiners, which gives Beijing an interest in a Hormuz settlement, but it has no visible role in brokering one.

### Russia

FACT: terms unchanged and stated at the highest level — talks "as soon as possible", but only if Ukraine leaves the rest of Donbas and accepts neutral status; Trump's proposals "require difficult decisions" for Russia (Putin at Valdai, 01.10, kremlin.ru; G4-019) [A/1]. Ryabkov (07.10): "no current plans" for the RU–US–UA format; bilateral contacts with the US continue (Interfax; G4-022) [B/1]. Peskov (05.10): "retaliation" for refinery strikes will continue (Interfax; G4-023) [B/1]. NYT (via Meduza, 05.10): the sale of Lukoil's foreign assets is part of the US–Russia talks; Peskov confirmed that energy cooperation is discussed but did not comment on the report (G4-020) [B/3]. Nuclear signalling over Kaliningrad (G1-029) [B/2]. Budget: 2027 defence 17.15 trn RUB (old plan 13.57 trn), 2026 deficit estimate 3.2% of GDP, new taxes on passive income from 01.01.2027 (G3-016) [B/2]; Putin admitted that Ukrainian refinery strikes cost about 1% of GDP (G1-051) [B/2]. On the flank: no incursion into Polish airspace after 23.09, but ships sunk in the Romanian and Bulgarian EEZs (attribution open) and drones and missiles over Moldova on 30.09 and 03.10 (Ukrainska Pravda, Moldova 1; G1-034) [B/3].

ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): Moscow budgets the cost of the war rather than reacting to it — it raises defence spending by about a quarter, finances it with taxes, borrowing and oil revenue, blocks the trilateral format and keeps a bilateral channel with Washington that includes economic offers. Pressure on NATO has shifted from Polish airspace to the Black Sea and to nuclear rhetoric — both below the Article 4 threshold.

### Iran (a regional actor of great-power scale in PIR-5)

FACT: the strait has become a formal instrument — the IRGC Navy route rule of 05.10 (G1-004) [C/3]; Brig. Gen. Naqdi (07.10): the strait "is closed", and the routes on the Omani side "will soon be blocked"; the army spokesman: Iran may conduct "pre-emptive operations" (Euronews citing IRNA and Fars; G1-005) [B/2]. Diplomatically: Iran's 7-day plan (lifting of the blockade and oil sanctions, frozen assets, a ceasefire including Lebanon, Hormuz reopened after 7 days, then nuclear talks) was rejected by Trump (Al Jazeera, Tehran Times; G4-002) [B/2]; the Majlis commission: no reopening until "seven conditions" are met (Tasnim, Mehr; G4-003) [B/2]. Pezeshkian called talks "futile" on 05.10 and a deal "not out of reach" on 07.10 (G4-005, G4-014). Domestic fracture: the impeachment motion against Araghchi (G4-009); Kayhan questions Ghalibaf's role as lead negotiator (Iran International; G4-011) [C/3]; the oil minister resigned on 05.10 (Al-Monitor citing state media; G2-013) [B/2]; the free-market rial near 2.7 mn per USD and annual inflation of 73.6% (Iran International; G3-056) [B/2]. The Supreme Leader communicates only in writing (leader.ir, 25.09 and 28.09; G4-012) [A/2].

ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): with its own exports near zero, Iran's remaining asset is control over others' exports through the strait; a clearance regime turns that asset into a bargaining chip that works without a deal. The negotiating mandate is now contested in Tehran, which weakens any commitment the foreign ministry can make.

### EU

FACT: the EU acts on three fronts. Sanctions: Coreper agreed on 07.10 the largest single expansion of the Russia list — 1,646 names, formal adoption by foreign ministers due on 12.10 (Euronews, Ukrinform; G3-022) [B/2]. Trade: Merz and Macron proposed a rapid-response instrument allowing the Commission to cut market access within days unless a qualified majority objects, aimed implicitly at China (France 24, 05.10; G2-043, G3-050) [B/2]; a hybrid-car safeguard is being prepared (G3-052) [B/3]. Enlargement: Hungary lifted its veto on opening clusters 2 and 3 with Ukraine on 07.10 (Euronews; G4-028) [B/2]; the Commission tabled its enlargement strategy (DW, 06–07.10; G4-082) [B/1]. Money: according to Bloomberg-based reports the Commission refused to front-load the 90 bn EUR loan to Ukraine (G3-053) [B/3]. Energy: the Commission opposes Poland's fuel VAT cut (G2-028); Brussels holds to the 01.01.2027 ban on long-term Russian LNG contracts (gCaptain, Euronews; G2-024) [B/2] — confirmed unchanged, early X 2026.

ASSESSMENT (confidence: medium): the EU is moving from reactive sanctions to defensive trade law. Its weak point remains money for Ukraine, not political will.
