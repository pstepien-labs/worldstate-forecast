# Actor models

Maintained by stage W (`prompts/W_world_model.md`, methodology v1.1 §3.3). Every claim cites the chains (`chains.csv`) or fact IDs that support it. A claim without evidence is marked ASSESSMENT with low confidence. Scored through CONDITIONAL questions (`registry/question_meta.csv`, `actor`): stage W copies the latest actor-model Brier from `01_scores.md` into each actor's "Track record" line.

Template for each actor:

- **Reacts to:** the moves of others that reliably draw a response
- **Repertoire:** escalate / mirror / absorb / delay / negotiate, with typical instruments by driver
- **Typical lag:** days from the trigger to the response, by instrument
- **Red lines:** declared (with source) and revealed (with chain)
- **Constraints now:** from the power ledger
- **Track record:** CONDITIONAL questions resolved, Brier, cohort
